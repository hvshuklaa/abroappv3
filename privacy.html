Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Abrovia is a free community for students planning or pursuing study abroad. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account data: email, username, accent colour.
  • Profile answers you give during onboarding (destination, budget range, test scores, interests).
  • Content you create: questions, answers, posts, stories, marketplace listings, ideas.
  • For Abrovia Seniors applying for verification: LinkedIn link, university email, proof of enrolment (stored privately) and bio.

How we use it

To run the app, personalise your roadmap, verify Seniors and keep the community safe. We do not sell your data and we do not take commissions from universities or agents.

Who can see what

Your username, accent, and public profile fields are visible to signed-in members. Onboarding answers and verification documents are private to you and Abrovia reviewers.

Processors

Data is stored with Supabase. Emails are sent through Supabase Auth.

Your rights

You can edit your profile in the app. To export or delete your account and data, email abrovia.edu@gmail.com.
